# Journal Entry Card Game

Open `game.html` in any browser to play. You can also play in chat with Claude.
Revise with `ENTRIES.md` first.

## How to play

1. Claude deals **one card at a time** (10 cards per round).
2. Each card shows a transaction and 4 options: **A, B, C, D**.
3. Reply with the letter only (e.g. `B`).
4. Claude replies ✅ or ❌ and, if wrong, explains the correct entry and *why*.
5. Scoring: ✅ = 10 points. 100 is a perfect round.
6. At the end Claude shows:
   - final score and grade
   - the **2–3 areas where you went wrong**, with the rule to remember
   - then a **new shuffled round** starts (type `stop` to end).

## Grades

| Score | Grade |
|---|---|
| 100 | 🏆 Master Accountant |
| 80–90 | ⭐ Very good |
| 60–70 | 👍 Getting there |
| below 60 | 📖 Revise `ENTRIES.md` and play again |

## Topics in the deck (25 entries in `ENTRIES.md`)

| Topic | What is tested |
|---|---|
| Sales cycle | credit sale + VAT Output, sales return, customer payment |
| Purchase cycle | credit purchase + VAT Input, purchase return, supplier payment |
| Advances | customer advance → sale, supplier advance → purchase |
| Accruals | accrued expense → invoice, accrued income → invoice |
| Prepayments | rent, insurance, visa: payment and monthly amortisation |
| Fixed assets | purchase, monthly depreciation |
| Monthly expenses | salary (WPS), marketing, office supplies, utilities, bank charges |

Mistakes are tracked by topic, so the end-of-round review points to the
exact entries to revise. Every card uses the same wording and amounts as `ENTRIES.md`, so she
repeats the same entries until they stick. Rounds after the first give extra cards from the
topics you got wrong.
